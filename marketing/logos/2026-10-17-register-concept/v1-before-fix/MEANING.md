# Veloxa SmartSchool logo: Register Path

The mark begins with a **class register**. Its vertical rule and three horizontal lines stand for the everyday records a school needs to keep clearly: attendance, class work and progress.

The top line rises toward a single dot. The dot represents a learner; the rising line represents using those records to notice a need, provide support and check progress. It reflects the product's working cycle: **record → understand → act → reassess**.

The open shape is intentionally simple so it remains recognizable on a phone, in a school sidebar and on printed material. Deep blue connects it to Veloxa SmartSchool's current interface and conveys a calm, dependable school tool.
