Before you say no

Every reason to wait, answered.

"We can't afford it."

There is nothing to afford. The school pays nothing, and the school is paid.

"Our teachers are not computer people."

If they can use WhatsApp, they can use this. Big buttons, plain English, on the phone in their pocket.

"Our children don't have devices."

They don't need any. Classwork stays on paper. Home learning runs on the parent's phone.

"What about our records?"

Your school is sealed off from every other school. Teachers see only their classes, parents only their own children, and children's names never go to the AI.

"We are already managing."

Managing costs your staff hours every week and leaves fees you can't see. This gives that time back.

"What if it doesn't suit us?"

Then you stop. There is no fee to lose and nothing to cancel.

Saying yes costs nothing.
Saying no costs hours every week, fees you can't track, and money that could come to your school.
